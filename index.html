Yoto Library Check 🎵

Lists every playlist in your Yoto family library with the date it was last changed, and checks a recording of a player's download list to show what is missing or out of date.

🔒 You sign in on Yoto's own website. This page can only view; it cannot change anything in your account.

It must be listed under "Allowed Callback URLs" in the Yoto developer app.

Library Check

    1. In the Yoto app, open Settings → My Players → your player → Audio download status.

    2. Start a screen recording, then scroll slowly down the whole list (about two seconds per screen) and stop the recording.

    3. Choose the recording below. Screenshots work too, if you only need to check a few. It is read on this device and nothing is uploaded.

    Tech info

    "Check players" asks Yoto what it will tell us about each player. Copy the result and paste it into the chat so I can see what is available.
